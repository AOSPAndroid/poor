# Real prediction markets

Identify exact contract ID, outcome, complete resolution rules, source and deadline. Retrieve the decisive official dataset or document. Record units, thresholds, observation period, timezone and handling of later revisions. Do not substitute news for the required settlement source.

Use current ask to assess purchase cost and current bid for possible exit; a last trade or indicative probability is not executable. Check depth, spread, fees and whether news is already reflected. Extremely lopsided contracts should not monopolize discovery merely because lifetime volume is high.
